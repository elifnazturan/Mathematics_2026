
# Exercise 2 – Addition and Scalar Multiplication

## Question

Given the matrices:

$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
-1 & 3 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
B =
\begin{pmatrix}
4 & -2 \\
0 & 5 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Calculate the following:

1. $A+B$
2. $A-B$
3. $3A-2B$

Also, explain why matrix addition is possible only for matrices of the same size.

## Solution

### 1. Matrix Addition

To add two matrices, I add their corresponding elements.

$$
A+B =
\begin{pmatrix}
1+4 & 2+(-2) \\
-1+0 & 3+5 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
A+B =
\begin{pmatrix}
5 & 0 \\
-1 & 8 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

### 2. Matrix Subtraction

For subtraction, I subtract each element of matrix B from the corresponding element of matrix A.

$$
A-B =
\begin{pmatrix}
1-4 & 2-(-2) \\
-1-0 & 3-5 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
A-B =
\begin{pmatrix}
-3 & 4 \\
-1 & -2 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

### 3. Scalar Multiplication

First, I multiply every element of A by 3 and every element of B by 2.

$$
3A =
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & 6 \\
-3 & 9 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
2B =
\begin{pmatrix}
8 & -4 \\
0 & 10 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Now I subtract the matrices:

$$
3A-2B =
\begin{pmatrix}
3-8 & 6-(-4) \\
-3-0 & 9-10 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
3A-2B =
\begin{pmatrix}
-5 & 10 \\
-3 & -1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

### 4. Why Must Matrices Have the Same Size?

Matrix addition is possible only when both matrices have the same number of rows and columns.

This is because each element of the first matrix must have a corresponding element in the second matrix.

In this exercise, both A and B have 2 rows and 2 columns. Therefore, addition and subtraction are possible.

## Final Answer

$$
A+B =
\begin{pmatrix}
5 & 0 \\
-1 & 8 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
A-B =
\begin{pmatrix}
-3 & 4 \\
-1 & -2 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
3A-2B =
\begin{pmatrix}
-5 & 10 \\
-3 & -1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Both matrices have the same size, so all the required operations are defined.
