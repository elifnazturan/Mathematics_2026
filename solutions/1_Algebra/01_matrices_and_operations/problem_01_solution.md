
# Exercise 1 – Matrix Size and Entries

## Question

Given the following matrices:

$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 3 \\
0 & 4 & 5 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
B =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
-2 & 3 \\
4 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

The tasks are:

1. State the sizes of matrices A and B.
2. Find the entries $a_{12}$, $a_{23}$, $b_{21}$, and $b_{32}$.
3. Write the second row of A and the first column of B as vectors.

## Solution

### 1. Matrix Sizes

The size of a matrix is determined by its number of rows and columns.

- Matrix A has 2 rows and 3 columns, so its size is $2 \times 3$.
- Matrix B has 3 rows and 2 columns, so its size is $3 \times 2$.

### 2. Matrix Entries

The first index indicates the row, and the second index indicates the column.

For matrix A:

- $a_{12} = -1$
- $a_{23} = 5$

For matrix B:

- $b_{21} = -2$
- $b_{32} = 1$

### 3. Rows and Columns as Vectors

The second row of matrix A is:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 4 & 5
\end{pmatrix}
$$

The first column of matrix B is:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 \\
-2 \\
4
\end{pmatrix}
$$

## Final Answer

- Size of A: $2 \times 3$
- Size of B: $3 \times 2$
- $a_{12} = -1$
- $a_{23} = 5$
- $b_{21} = -2$
- $b_{32} = 1$

Second row of A: $(0, 4, 5)$

First column of B: $(1, -2, 4)^T$
